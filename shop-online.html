How to order

Please email your order to: orders@nakano.nz.

Please include:

  • full name and delivery address
  • a declaration that you're over 18: "I declare I'm over 18 years of age"

We'll then send you an invoice with internet banking payment instructions. We'll send your order by courier once we've received payment.

Shipping

Order 3 or more bottles and standard delivery is included.

North Island $6.99 1-2 working days
South Island $8.99 3-4 working days
South Island express $12.99 1-2 working days
Rural delivery +$4.99 allow another 2-3 working days
