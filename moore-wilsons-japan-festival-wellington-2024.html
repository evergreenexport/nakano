Japan Festival Wellington at
Moore Wilson’s Wine Beer & Spirits

There'll be a special sake and umeshu (plum wine) display at Moore Wilson's Wine Beer & Spirits on Tory Street for the duration of the Japan Festival Wellington. This will run from Sat 14 Sept to Sun 22 Sept 2024. The store is open each day from 8.30am to 6pm.

Plus an in-store sake and umeshu tasting on Saturday 21 September from 12.00pm to 4.00pm (age restricted 18+)

Sponsored by the Nakano Brewery.
